CREASEFIRE

Air hockey across the fold.

Creasefire is fast air hockey built for the foldable iPhone Duo. The center line of the rink sits right on the crease, so two players can face off with the phone open between them. Bend the phone a little and the rink tilts into a valley.

  • Two players on one phone, or play the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard
  • Fold physics that follow the hinge angle live
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data collection
