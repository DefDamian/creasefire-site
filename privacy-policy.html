Creasefire Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Applies to the Creasefire app for iPhone and iPad, offered in the United States.

The short version: Creasefire does not collect any personal information. There are no Creasefire accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third party code that collects data. Your settings and your win record stay on your device. If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are shared with Game Center, which Apple runs.

Who we are

Creasefire is made by Damian Cedeno ("we", "us"). You can reach us at dca.support@icloud.com.

Information we collect

None. Creasefire has no servers of its own and does not send any information about you or your device to us. The only information that leaves your device is the optional Game Center data described below, which goes to Apple.

Information that stays on your device

  • Settings such as sound, haptics, fold physics, CPU difficulty and goals to win.
  • Your win and loss record against the computer opponent.

This is saved only on your device so the game remembers your choices. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also reset your record in Settings inside the game.

Game Center (optional)

Creasefire supports Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is optional: the whole game works without it. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Creasefire sends the following to Game Center:

  • Your lifetime number of goals scored against the computer opponent.
  • Your best win streak against the computer on each difficulty.
  • Your progress on achievements, such as your first goal, first win and shutouts.

Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Depending on your Game Center settings, other players may see your Game Center nickname next to your scores on leaderboards, and we can see the same leaderboard entries (nickname and score) that any player can. We never receive your name, email address or Apple Account details, and we do not combine leaderboard data with anything else. To stop sharing, sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings, or change who can see your profile there.

The fold sensor on iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo, the game reads how far the phone is folded so it can tilt the virtual rink and place the center line on the crease. This reading is used live during play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Age range in certain U.S. states

Some U.S. states, including Texas, require apps to check a user's age category through the App Store. Where Apple tells the app that such a law applies to you, Creasefire uses Apple's Declared Age Range feature to ask for your age range (for example, "13 or older"). Apple shares only the range, never your birthday, and you or a parent can manage this in your Apple Account settings. Creasefire does not store your age range, does not send it anywhere, and does not lock any part of the game based on it. The whole game is suitable for all ages.

Children

Creasefire is a general audience game suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13. Game Center, including whether a child can use it, is managed by Apple and by parents through Screen Time and Family Sharing. If you believe a child has given us personal information in some other way, such as by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Information Apple may collect

Apple runs the App Store and may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example when you download the app. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and app usage statistics. We use these only to fix bugs and improve the game. We cannot identify you from them. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose personal information, because we do not have any. We do not use your information for targeted advertising. Optional Game Center scores are handled by Apple as described above.

Your privacy rights, including California residents

Laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act and the privacy laws of other U.S. states give residents rights to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, sell or share. You can still contact us with any request and we will answer it.

Do Not Track: Creasefire does not track you across apps or websites, so it treats every user as if a Do Not Track signal were on.

Security

Your settings and records stay on your device and are protected by its own security, such as your passcode and Face ID or Touch ID. Optional Game Center data is sent to Apple over Apple's own secure connection.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change ever means the app would start collecting personal information, we will update the app's App Store privacy details first and explain the change in the app.
