Common questions

What do I need to play?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Creasefire is made for iPhone Duo and works on every iPhone that runs iOS 26.

How do two players play?

Choose Two Players. Open the phone flat on a table between you. Each player drags on their own side of the center line. On iPhone Duo the center line sits right on the fold, so each player gets one half of the screen.

What is fold physics?

On iPhone Duo, bend the phone a little like a book and the rink becomes a valley, so the puck slides toward the crease. The more you fold, the stronger the pull. Turn it off in Settings if you prefer a flat rink.

The game paused by itself. Why?

The game pauses when the screen changes shape (for example when you open, close or rotate the phone, or use split view), when you close the phone, or when you leave the app. Tap Resume when you are ready.

How do I reset my record?

Open Settings from the main menu and tap Reset record.

Does Creasefire collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
